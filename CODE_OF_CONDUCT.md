# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

Aresys publishes open source software to encourage collaboration between
employees, partners, and independent contributors. Productive collaboration
depends on an environment where people can discuss technical work openly,
disagree respectfully, and participate without harassment or intimidation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies equally to Aresys employees and to external
contributors participating in Aresys open source projects. It applies to
participation in Aresys open source projects across project spaces, community
communication, and related online or in-person events. It also applies when
someone represents, or could reasonably be seen as representing, an Aresys
project or the Aresys organization, including through personal accounts
associated with Aresys repositories, teams, or organizations.

## Conduct Expectations

Aresys expects contributors to work with courtesy, professionalism, and
respect. Productive technical collaboration depends on discussing ideas openly,
giving constructive feedback, welcoming questions, and helping others
understand project decisions, workflows, and technical context.

Differences in experience, background, perspective, and communication style are
a normal part of open source work and should be treated with respect.

Technical disagreement is welcome, but it must remain focused on the work and
expressed in a professional way. Contributors should be free to challenge code,
design choices, documentation, and process, but they must do so without
attacking, belittling, or dismissing other people. Debate is a normal part of
open source collaboration, but personal insults, humiliation, and hostile
language are unacceptable.

Behavior that intimidates, harasses, targets, or excludes others has no place
in Aresys open source projects. This includes repeated unwanted contact,
threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized comments or conduct, sharing
private information or communications without permission, retaliation against
someone who raises a concern, and encouraging others to behave in these ways.

## Resolution of Issues

Aresys encourages contributors to resolve concerns early, calmly, and
respectfully whenever possible. Open source communities are made of people, and
people can make mistakes. When appropriate, we prefer to begin by clarifying
the issue, listening to its impact, and giving contributors the opportunity to
correct their behavior.

Repeated misconduct is not tolerated. If a person continues the same behavior
after being warned, or if the conduct is serious from the start, Aresys may
take stronger action, including restricting participation, suspending access to
project spaces, or banning the person from the community.

For serious issues that cannot be raised safely through normal community
channels or whenever a contributor prefers to remain anonymous reports can be
submitted through [Aresys' confidential whistleblowing platform].

## AI Assisted Contributions

Aresys encourages innovation and welcomes the responsible use of AI tools. We
believe they can improve the quality of contributions, but each contributor
remains responsible for what they submit. Anyone using AI tools should review
and revise their work before posting it, and should take full ownership of the
final contribution. Misuse of AI tools, including submitting unchecked or
misleading content, may be considered a violation of this Code of Conduct.

[Aresys' confidential whistleblowing platform]: https://www.aresys.it/about/whistleblowing-procedure/
